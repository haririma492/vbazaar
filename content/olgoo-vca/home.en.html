VCA™  ·  Virtual Commercial Address

VCA turns e-commerce from scrolling into walking in.

VCA — Virtual Commercial Address — brings back what e-commerce has lost: the vibe of a shopping district, the closeness of stores, discovery, serendipity, and even nostalgia. Instead of searching for exactly what you already know you want, you can virtually arrive at a real commercial district or shopping centre, wander through it, notice stores you weren’t looking for, step inside, discover products, and buy — much as you would in the physical world.

VCA is more than a single virtual store; each store is a destination within a much larger spatial commerce network. We’re putting place, proximity and accidental discovery back into e-commerce — creating the bridge between physical commerce and e-commerce.

The bridge between physical commerce and e-commerce.

E-commerce made shopping extraordinarily efficient. But in doing so, it removed something fundamental.

Place.

In the physical world, you don’t always begin shopping by knowing exactly what you want. You go somewhere.

You walk down a street, through a shopping centre or a market You notice another store Something in a window catches your eye You remember a place you used to visit You discover something you never would have searched for

That is serendipity, and today’s e-commerce largely eliminated it.

VCA adds the place back to e-commerce —

along with its feeling, its vibe, its nostalgia, its serendipity, and the incidental shopping that comes with simply being there.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is a 3D virtual store?

A 3D copy of a store that shoppers can walk into online, browse and buy from. With VCA, each virtual store also sits on its real street or in its real mall, next to its neighbours.

Is this a virtual tour of my store?

It starts like one: your store is captured in 360° and 3D, like a retail store virtual tour. The difference is that customers can shop it — tap a price tag, add to the basket and check out — and walk next door to the neighbouring stores.

What is a Virtual Commercial Address (VCA)?

A destination in the digital world that corresponds to a real commercial place — a street, mall or store — so people can arrive online, walk through it, step into stores and buy.

How is VCA different from other virtual stores or a 360° tour?

A tour shows one place, and most virtual stores stand alone; VCA connects places. Stores sit beside stores on real streets, and every product can be tapped and bought — it’s shopping, not just a view.

Do shoppers need an app or a VR headset?

No. VCA runs in any modern browser on a computer, phone or tablet. VR headsets work too, and on a big screen it’s even better.

Who can join a VCA pilot?

Store owners, business improvement areas (BIAs), landlords and mall operators. We’re choosing our first pilot districts now.

Is VCA patented?

A patent application for VCA has been filed with the Canadian Intellectual Property Office (CIPO). VCA™ is a trademark of Olgoo Enterprise Services Inc.

How long does it take to set up a virtual store?

About two weeks for a single store with VCA Storefront. A street, plaza or mall is planned with you as a pilot.

How much does a virtual store cost?

A single walk-in virtual store (VCA Storefront) starts from $3,500. Streets, malls and founding districts are quoted with you, based on their size.

How do shoppers buy in a VCA virtual store?

They walk in, tap a product’s price tag and add it to the basket, then check out online.
